# TitraSims — Student Guide

TitraSims is an augmented-reality titration lab you run on your phone. You point
the camera at a printed marker in your activity book, virtual lab equipment
appears on the page, and you work through the procedure one step at a time.

There are two procedures, each with ten stages (*tahapan*):

- **Titrasi Bebas Air (TBA)** — non-aqueous titration, assaying a caffeine
  sample against a perchloric-acid standard.
- **Titrasi Kompleksometri (TK)** — complexometric titration, assaying a CaCO₃
  sample against an EDTA standard.

> **The app is in Indonesian.** This guide is in English, but every on-screen
> label is quoted exactly as it appears so you can match them up.

## What you need

- **An Android phone or tablet** running Android 10 or newer, with a working
  rear camera. The device must support Google ARCore — most phones from 2019
  onward do.
- **Your activity book**, which contains the twenty printed markers. Each
  tahapan has its own marker; they are not interchangeable.
- **A flat surface** — a desk or table — to lay the book on.
- **Even, reasonably bright light.** Direct glare on the page and dim rooms are
  the two most common reasons tracking fails.

You do not need an internet connection to use the app.

## Getting started

1. Install the app on your device (your instructor will provide the APK or a
   store link) and grant it **camera permission** when it asks. Without camera
   access nothing will appear.
2. Open the app. You land on the home screen — the title *TITRA…* with the
   tagline *"Explore, Scan, Learn — Titrasi dalam Genggamanmu!"*
3. Tap **Mulai** (Start).
4. Choose your procedure under **JENIS TITRASI** (titration type): **Titrasi
   Bebas Air** or **Titrasi Kompleksometri**.
5. You now see that procedure's list of ten tahapan. Tap **Tahap 1** to begin.

Before your first session it is worth reading **Cara Bermain** (How to Play) on
the home screen — it covers the same gestures described below, with pictures.

## The screens

| On screen | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Mulai** | Start — goes to the procedure chooser |
| **Cara Bermain** | How to Play — the built-in gesture tutorial |
| **Informasi Tim** | Team Information — credits |
| **JENIS TITRASI** | Titration type — pick TBA or Kompleksometri |
| **Tahapan** / **10 Level** | The list of ten stages for the procedure you chose |
| **Reset** | Erases your progress — see [below](#resetting-your-progress) |

Stages you have not unlocked yet appear **dimmed** and will not respond when
tapped. You unlock the next stage by finishing the one before it, so you always
work in order.

## Controls

### Gestures

| Gesture | What it does |
|---|---|
| **One finger, drag across an object** | Moves the object |
| **Two fingers, drag** | Rotates the object |
| **Two fingers, pinch** | Makes the object bigger or smaller |
| **One finger, draw circles** | Stirs — used when a step asks you to *aduk* (mix) the Erlenmeyer |

Not every object responds to every gesture. Some equipment is deliberately
fixed in place, or only slides along one direction — a burette clamp that only
moves up and down, for example. If an object will not move, that is intentional
and the step does not need it to.

### Buttons during a stage

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Next arrow** | Confirms the current step and moves to the next one. It only appears once the step is ready to be advanced. |
| **Selesai** | Finish — appears after the last step. Tapping it completes the tahapan, saves your progress and returns you to the stage list. |
| **i** (info) | Opens the theory panel for this tahapan: the equipment and materials list, and the numbered procedure. |
| **Speech-bubble button** | Shows or hides the narration panel with the current step's instruction. |
| **Play / Stop** | Replays or stops the animation for the current step. Only appears on steps that have one. |
| **Back arrow** | Leaves the tahapan and returns to the stage list. **Your progress in that tahapan is not saved** — you start it again from step 1. |

Some steps show extra buttons with amounts on them, such as **1 ml** and
**10 ml**. Tap these to add liquid in those increments until you reach the volume
the instruction asks for.

## Running a tahapan

1. **Tap the tahapan** in the list. The camera opens and the app asks you to
   scan.
2. **Point the camera at that tahapan's marker** in your activity book. Hold the
   phone roughly 20–30 cm above the page so the whole marker is in frame.
3. **The equipment appears on the page** and the first step begins
   automatically. A short narration plays and the instruction appears in a panel
   at the bottom.
4. **Do what the step asks.** Depending on the step this might mean watching an
   animation, tapping an amount button, dragging a piece of equipment into
   position, or stirring with a circular finger motion.
5. **Tap the next arrow** when it appears to move on. Some steps advance by
   themselves once their animation finishes — if no arrow appears, just wait.
6. **Repeat** until the last step, then tap **Selesai**.

The narration panel hides itself a few seconds after the marker is found so it
does not block your view. Tap the speech-bubble button to bring it back, or the
**i** button if you want to re-read the whole procedure.

Keep the marker in view the whole time. If you move the camera away, the
equipment disappears and **SCAN MARKERNYA** ("scan the marker") appears — point
back at the page and everything resumes where you left off.

## What each tahapan asks you to do

Both procedures follow the same ten-stage shape. The chemistry differs.

### Titrasi Bebas Air (TBA)

| # | Tahapan | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Preparasi Alat dan Bahan | Review the equipment and materials — glassware, caffeine sample, crystal-violet indicator, perchloric acid standard |
| 2 | Preparasi Buret | Check the burette is dry, rinse it with the perchloric acid standard, then fill it |
| 3 | Preparasi Larutan Baku Primer | Add 700 mg potassium biphthalate and 50 mL glacial acetic acid to the Erlenmeyer, then mix |
| 4 | Preparasi Larutan Blanko | Add 40 mL acetic anhydride and 80 mL benzene to the Erlenmeyer, then mix |
| 5 | Penimbangan Sampel | Weigh out 400 mg of caffeine on the analytical balance |
| 6 | Preparasi Larutan Sampel | Add the sample, 40 mL acetic anhydride and 80 mL benzene, then dissolve by mixing |
| 7 | Penambahan Indikator | Draw crystal-violet indicator with the pipette and add 2–3 drops to each Erlenmeyer |
| 8 | Simulasi Pembakuan Secara Triplo (3×) | Standardise against the potassium biphthalate — record start and end burette volumes, three times |
| 9 | Simulasi Titrasi Blanko | Titrate the blank — record start and end volumes |
| 10 | Simulasi Titrasi Sampel Secara Triplo (3×) | Titrate the caffeine sample — record start and end volumes, three times |

### Titrasi Kompleksometri (TK)

| # | Tahapan | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Preparasi Alat dan Bahan | Review the equipment and materials — glassware, CaCO₃ sample, hydroxynaphthol blue indicator, EDTA standard |
| 2 | Preparasi Buret | Rinse the burette with the EDTA standard, then fill it |
| 3 | Preparasi Larutan Baku Primer | Add 200 mg CaCO₃, 10 mL distilled water and 2 mL HCl, mix, then add 100 mL water, 30 mL EDTA and 15 mL NaOH, and mix again |
| 4 | Preparasi Larutan Blanko | Build the blank from distilled water, a few drops of 3N HCl, 100 mL water and 15 mL 1N NaOH, then mix |
| 5 | Penimbangan Sampel | Weigh out 200 mg of the CaCO₃ sample |
| 6 | Preparasi Larutan Sampel | Add the weighed sample, water, 3N HCl, 100 mL water and 15 mL NaOH, then dissolve by mixing |
| 7 | Penambahan Indikator | Add 300 mg of pre-weighed hydroxynaphthol blue indicator to the Erlenmeyer |
| 8 | Simulasi Pembakuan Secara Triplo (3×) | Standardise against the CaCO₃ — record start and end volumes, three times |
| 9 | Simulasi Titrasi Blanko | Titrate the blank — record start and end volumes |
| 10 | Simulasi Titrasi Sampel Secara Triplo (3×) | Titrate the CaCO₃ sample — record start and end volumes, three times |

## Recording your results

Stages 8, 9 and 10 are the ones that produce data. Each of them tells you to:

1. **Read and note the starting burette volume** *before* you titrate.
2. Run the titration until the endpoint — the solution changes colour.
3. **Read and note the final burette volume** *after* you titrate.

Write both readings in your **activity book**, not in the app. TitraSims does
not store your volumes or do the arithmetic for you — working out the titre and
the assay result is the point of the exercise.

"Triplo" means the stage runs three times. Record all three pairs of readings
separately.

## Troubleshooting

**Nothing appears when I point at the marker.**
Check that the whole marker is inside the frame and the page is flat — a curled
or folded page will not track. Move to better light, and avoid glare from a
window or ceiling light falling directly on the page. Hold the phone steady,
roughly 20–30 cm away.

**The equipment keeps disappearing.**
That is tracking being lost. Keep the marker in frame and avoid covering part of
it with your hand while you gesture. Rest the phone against something if holding
it steady is awkward.

**I scanned a marker but the wrong thing appeared — or nothing did.**
Each tahapan has its own marker. Make sure you are pointing at the marker for
the tahapan you actually opened.

**The stage I want is greyed out.**
Stages unlock in order. Finish the previous one — all the way to **Selesai** —
to unlock the next.

**I tapped Back and lost my place.**
Leaving a tahapan with the back arrow discards progress *within* that tahapan.
Tahapan you have already completed stay unlocked.

**The next arrow never appears.**
Some steps advance on their own when their animation ends — wait a moment.
Others are waiting for you to do something first: add the right volume with the
amount buttons, stir the flask, or move a piece of equipment into place. Re-read
the instruction in the narration panel, or tap **i** for the full procedure.

**The app asks for camera permission again.**
Grant it. AR cannot work without the camera.

### Resetting your progress

The **Reset** button clears your progress for **both** procedures and cannot be
undone. Progress is stored on the device itself, not in an account — so on a
shared tablet you are picking up wherever the previous student left off, and
resetting wipes their progress too. Check with your instructor before using it.
